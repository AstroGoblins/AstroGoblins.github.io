Frequently asked questions

I bought "No Ads" – how do I get it back on a new iPhone?

Open the game → Settings (⚙️) → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I lost my progress / I have a new iPhone.

If you signed in with Apple in the game (Settings → Account), your progress is backed up to your iCloud Keychain and comes back when you sign in again with the same Apple Account. Progress of guest players is stored only on that device.

How do I get a refund?

All purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Star Shards from a refunded pack are removed from the game.

How do I delete my data?

In the game: Settings → Reset progress. This deletes your progress on the device and the iCloud backup. Deleting the app removes the local data too. See the Privacy Policy.

How can I change my ad choices?

In the EU/UK: Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. Tracking can be turned off any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Rewards or notifications don't arrive.

Check that notifications for Astro Goblins are allowed in iOS Settings, and that you have an internet connection for ad rewards. If it still doesn't work, write to us.
