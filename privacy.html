Privacy Policy

Astro Goblins · Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Hrvatski

This policy explains what data the iOS game Astro Goblins uses and why. The game is made by mmGames (Martino Majić), Croatia, who is responsible for your data ("controller" under the GDPR). Contact: notepad-flairs-3u@icloud.com.

Short version: we run no servers of our own and do not sell your data. Your game progress stays on your device (and in your own iCloud if you sign in). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which uses data as described below and only with the consent required by law.

1. Game progress (on your device)

Your progress (gold, levels, managers, settings, purchases already received) is stored on your iPhone. It is not sent to us.

2. Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Apple", Apple gives the game an anonymous user identifier and, the first time only, your first name. They are used to back up your progress in your own iCloud Keychain, so you can keep playing after reinstalling or on a new iPhone. We never receive your email address. You can stop using Sign in with Apple at any time in iOS Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The game stores Apple's transaction IDs on your device so each purchase is delivered once, and so Star Shards of a refunded purchase can be removed.

4. Advertising – Google AdMob

The game shows ads (a banner and optional rewarded videos) from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, device and app information and how you interact with ads.

  • Consent (EU/EEA/UK/Switzerland): on first launch Google's consent form asks for your choice. You can change it any time in the game: Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • The game and all rewards work exactly the same whatever you choose.
  • Players who buy "No Ads" see no ads.

More: How Google uses data from partner apps · Google Privacy Policy.

5. Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the game reports leaderboard scores (galaxy reached, Dark Matter, meteors caught) and achievement progress to Apple. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example "Expedition is back!"). No push server is used and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

7. Legal bases (GDPR)

Playing the game and backing up your progress: performance of the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)). Personalised ads and the advertising identifier: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention by Google: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

8. How long data is kept

Game data stays on your device and in your iCloud until you reset progress or delete the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

9. Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to processing, ask for data portability and withdraw consent. Most data never leaves your device or your Apple account, so you can delete it yourself: in the game, Settings → Reset progress deletes your progress and its iCloud backup; deleting the app removes the rest. For anything else write to notepad-flairs-3u@icloud.com. You may also complain to a data protection authority – in Croatia the AZOP (azop.hr).

10. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and it will be deleted.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Ukratko: nemamo vlastite servere i ne prodajemo tvoje podatke. Napredak u igri ostaje na tvom uređaju (i u tvom iCloudu ako se prijaviš). Reklame prikazuje Google AdMob, koji podatke koristi kako je opisano niže i samo uz pristanak koji traži zakon.

1. Napredak u igri (na uređaju)

Napredak (zlato, razine, menadžeri, postavke, primljene kupnje) sprema se na tvom iPhoneu i ne šalje se nama.

2. Prijava s Appleom (neobavezno)

Ako odabereš "Prijava s Appleom", Apple igri daje anonimni identifikator i, samo prvi put, tvoje ime. Koriste se za sigurnosnu kopiju napretka u tvom iCloud privjesku ključeva, da možeš nastaviti igrati nakon ponovne instalacije ili na novom iPhoneu. Tvoju e-mail adresu nikad ne dobivamo.

3. Kupnje u aplikaciji

Kupnje u potpunosti obrađuje Apple; ne dobivamo podatke o plaćanju. Igra na uređaju sprema Appleove ID-ove transakcija kako bi svaka kupnja bila isporučena jednom i kako bi se kod povrata novca uklonili pripadajući kristali.

4. Reklame – Google AdMob

Igra prikazuje reklame (banner i neobavezne video reklame za nagradu) tvrtke Google. Za prikaz, mjerenje i ograničavanje reklama te sprječavanje prijevara Google može prikupljati podatke kao što su reklamni identifikator uređaja (IDFA, samo ako dopustiš praćenje), IP adresa, približna lokacija, podaci o uređaju i aplikaciji te interakcija s reklamama.

  • Pristanak (EU/EGP/UK/Švicarska): pri prvom pokretanju Googleov obrazac traži tvoj izbor, a možeš ga promijeniti u igri: Postavke → Privatnost → Postavke privatnosti za reklame.
  • Praćenje: iOS pita smije li te igra pratiti. Ako odbiješ, IDFA se ne dijeli. Izbor možeš promijeniti u iOS postavkama → Privatnost i sigurnost → Praćenje.
  • Igra i sve nagrade rade jednako bez obzira na tvoj izbor. Tko kupi "Bez reklama", ne vidi reklame.

5. Game Center (neobavezno)

Ako si prijavljen u Apple Game Center, igra Appleu šalje rezultate na ljestvicama i napredak u postignućima.

6. Obavijesti

Ako ih dopustiš, igra podsjetnike zakazuje na tvom uređaju. Ne koristi se server i ništa se ne šalje nama.

7. Pravne osnove (GDPR)

Igra i sigurnosna kopija: izvršenje usluge koju tražiš (čl. 6. st. 1. t. b). Personalizirane reklame: tvoj pristanak (čl. 6. st. 1. t. a), koji možeš povući u svakom trenutku. Nepersonalizirane reklame i sprječavanje prijevara: legitimni interes (čl. 6. st. 1. t. f).

8. Tvoja prava

Imaš pravo na pristup, ispravak i brisanje podataka, ograničenje obrade, prigovor, prenosivost i povlačenje pristanka. U igri: Postavke → Resetiraj napredak briše napredak i njegovu iCloud kopiju; brisanjem aplikacije uklanjaš ostalo. Za sve ostalo piši na notepad-flairs-3u@icloud.com. Pritužbu možeš podnijeti Agenciji za zaštitu osobnih podataka (azop.hr).

9. Djeca

Igra nije namijenjena djeci mlađoj od 13 godina i svjesno ne prikupljamo njihove podatke.
